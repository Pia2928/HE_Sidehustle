Your next steps

A short guide (about 5 minutes) to turning your chosen idea into a real side business. Open any section that interests you.

1. Check that people want it
  • Describe your idea in one sentence to five people who could be customers.
  • Ask what they use today and what annoys them about it, not whether they like your idea.
  • Look for existing businesses offering something similar. Competition often means there is demand.
2. Find your first customers
  • Start with people you can reach easily: friends, colleagues, local groups, online communities.
  • Offer a small trial or first version at a fair price to learn what people value.
  • Write down where your ideal customers spend time, online and offline.
3. Work out costs and price
  • List what you need to start: tools, materials, software, a simple website.
  • Estimate your cost per sale and the price customers would pay.
  • Ask: how many sales a month would make this worth my time?
4. Fit it around your job
  • Block a few fixed hours each week for the business.
  • Check your employment contract for rules about outside work or conflicts of interest.
  • Choose the smallest version of the idea you can test in a month.
5. Sort out the basics
  • Read HMRC's guidance on side income to see whether and when you need to register.
  • Keep simple records of income and costs from the first sale.
  • Check whether your idea needs insurance, licences or permissions.
